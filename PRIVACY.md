# SERP Rank Tracker – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

SERP Rank Tracker is a Chrome extension that helps Amazon sellers analyze Amazon search results for their keywords.

## Summary

SERP Rank Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or user data. Everything the extension saves stays in your own Chrome browser on your own computer.

## What the extension stores, and where

The extension saves the following in Chrome's local extension storage on your device:

- **Your settings:** your brand name, your own ASINs, and the competitor ASIN list you paste in.
- **Scan results:** when you click "Scan pages 1–3", the search results of those pages (ASINs, titles, ranks, prices, ratings, badges) and product details for the top 40 organic results (title, highlights, bullet points, best seller rank, category path, rating, number of ratings, review summary and top reviews).

This data is used only to show rank tables, brand share and competitor flags inside the extension, and to create CSV files that you download yourself.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third-party server.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies of its own, or advertising.
- It does not read or store your Amazon account details, orders, payment information or passwords.
- It does not run on any website other than Amazon search result pages and Amazon product pages.

## Pages the extension reads

The extension reads Amazon search result pages and Amazon product pages on amazon.com, amazon.ca, amazon.co.uk and amazon.com.mx. Automatic reading of pages 1–3 and of product pages happens only after you click "Scan pages 1–3 + details". These requests go directly from your browser to Amazon, the same as visiting the pages yourself.

## Deleting your data

Open the extension, go to **Settings**, and click **Delete all saved snapshots and product details**. Removing the extension from Chrome also deletes everything it stored.
